About this Project
Digital Heritage,
Reimagined
This project was built to empower researchers, scholars, and history enthusiasts exploring the vast collections of the National Archives of Pakistan. Traditionally, finding a specific reference in these archives meant spending countless hours manually reading through fragile physical books. We created this digital library to solve that problem — by making these historical texts fully searchable.

Users can simply type a word or phrase, instantly discover which books contain it, and seamlessly read the exact scanned pages where the information is located. Our goal is to bridge the gap between historical preservation and modern technology, making Pakistan's rich history easily accessible to anyone who seeks it.
